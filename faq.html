Getting started

The basics

What is CartChkr? #

CartChkr is a free app for iPhone and Android that tells Australian shoppers whether Woolies or Coles is cheaper for the things they actually buy — this week, not on average.

There are two ways in. Do a Quick Price Check: snap a product or a receipt, or type or dictate your shopping list, and CartChkr prices every item at both stores. Or scan your live Woolies or Coles cart, if you've already filled one. Either way you get an item-by-item comparison, and anything cheaper at the other store can be moved across for you.

It works in both directions — start at Woolies and compare to Coles, or start at Coles and compare to Woolies. There's also a Chrome extension for desktop, but the phone app is where CartChkr is most capable.

What does it run on? #

Phone — the main way to use CartChkr. A free app for iPhone and Android, available in Australia. Everything described on this page works there.

Desktop. A Chrome extension that also runs in any Chromium browser — Microsoft Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi. It's a smaller tool than the app: it scans a Woolies cart and compares to Coles, one direction only, and it has no CartChkr Cart and no Price History. It doesn't run on Safari or Firefox, or in any mobile browser, because Chrome for iOS and Android don't support extensions at all. Firefox support is on the roadmap.

Both stores restrict their own websites to Australian IP addresses, so CartChkr needs you to be in Australia and off a VPN. See the overseas question.

Do I need to sign up for a CartChkr account? #

No. There is no CartChkr account, no email signup, no password to remember. We deliberately never built one.

For a Quick Price Check — photo, receipt, typed list or dictated list — you don't need to sign in to anything at all. To scan a live store cart, or to move items into one, you sign in to Woolworths and Coles on each store's own secure page, the same as you would to shop. CartChkr never sees those passwords.

How do I get started? #

On your phone: download CartChkr from the App Store or Google Play and open it. The fastest first run is a Quick Price Check — tap Scan a Photo and point the camera at a receipt, or tap Type it or Say it and add a few items. No sign-in needed for either.

When you want to scan a real cart or move items across, tap the Woolies or Coles tab at the bottom and sign in there.

On a computer: open the Chrome Web Store page and click Add to Chrome. Pin it to your toolbar (puzzle-piece icon → pin next to CartChkr), open Woolies or Coles, click the CartChkr icon, and the side panel appears.

Can I see how it works before signing in to anything? #

Yes, two ways. A Quick Price Check uses live prices and needs no sign-in — that's the real thing, not a demo.

And if you want to see the whole flow without touching a store at all, Demo Mode walks you through scan → compare → Move → Price History on a sample 32-item cart. No login, no purchase, no Australian IP required. It's in More.

Why does it need access to woolworths.com.au and coles.com.au? #

To compare prices CartChkr has to read your trolley and look up each item at the other store. That's the whole purpose of the access. It can't see anything on any other site, it never reads your passwords or payment details, and it doesn't send your cart anywhere — see the privacy section.

Using it

Checking, matching, moving

What is the CartChkr Cart? #

It's a shopping list that knows what things cost at both stores — so you can compare a shop before you've done it, instead of filling a trolley at one chain and hoping.

Four ways to put things in it, and you can mix them freely:

  • Scan a receipt — photograph a Woolies or Coles docket and the whole shop comes in at once.
  • Photograph a product — point the camera at the front of a pack; CartChkr reads the label.
  • Type it — "milk 2L, bread, a dozen eggs". Commas or the word "plus" both separate items.
  • Say it — tap Voice and dictate with your keyboard's microphone. See the microphone question.

Every line gets priced at Woolies and at Coles, with a running subtotal for each so you can see which store wins overall. You pick a Preferred Store — the one you intend to shop at — and Move All loads items into that store's real cart when you're ready, either just the cheaper ones or the lot. Clear Cart empties it and starts again.

Building and pricing a CartChkr Cart is free. Moving items into a store cart is part of the paid unlock.

How do I scan a cart I've already filled? #

Fill your trolley at Woolies or Coles as normal, then open CartChkr and tap Woolies cart or Coles cart under "Check your live Woolies or Coles carts". CartChkr reads every item, finds the matching product at the other store, and shows you the comparison with a subtotal for each chain.

The tile tells you whether you're signed in before you tap. How long it takes depends on how many items you have, because each one is a separate lookup at the other store — leave CartChkr open and it'll work through them. The first scan after an app update is slower again — see why.

What do the grey chips on an item mean? #

They're how CartChkr tells you it isn't certain, instead of quietly pretending it is. There are four, and you'll only ever see one per item — the most important one.

  • Check match — "Closest match only — check before moving." This is general doubt: we may have the wrong product entirely. It deliberately doesn't claim to know what's wrong, because it doesn't.
  • Check quantity — you asked for several of something and the match is a multipack. If you asked for 6 bananas and the match is a 5-pack, that's 30 bananas. We say so rather than multiply silently.
  • Size may differ — right product, different amount. If we could normalise the sizes, the item still counts toward your saving; if we couldn't, the chip says "not counted" and we leave it out of the total.
  • Different brand — that chain stocks none of your brand at all, so the closest thing is somebody else's. A difference in who made it, not necessarily in what you pay.

Tap an item to see the reason spelled out. If a chip is on an item we got wrong, tap Wrong match? and pick the right product — we'll remember it.

Why are some items marked "no match"? #

Being straight with you: "no match" currently covers two different situations, and the wording doesn't yet tell them apart. We know, and we're fixing it.

  • We couldn't find the product — the other chain genuinely doesn't stock it, the two stores name it very differently, or the search came back empty.
  • We found it but couldn't give you a verdict — usually because the sizes don't compare cleanly, so putting a dollar figure on it would be misleading.

The commonest real causes are own-brand products with no equivalent at the other chain, fresh produce where the two stores use different internal codes, and short generic searches like "chicken breast" where we struggle to pin down what you meant.

Either way: tap Wrong match?. You'll get the closest alternatives we found, and when there are none the button becomes Search {store} store so you can look yourself. Picking a product there teaches CartChkr the pairing for next time.

CartChkr matched the wrong product #

Tap Wrong match? on that item and choose the right product from the alternatives. CartChkr saves the pairing and uses it on every future scan. Thumbs-down blocks a bad pairing so it never comes back.

Two places we're honestly still weak, so you know what to watch:

  • Short, generic searches. "chicken breast" is harder for us than "chicken breast fillet 500g" — the fewer words you give us, the more we have to guess. This is our top engineering priority right now.
  • Flavour variants. Fixed on 30 October 2026. Ask for a plain yoghurt and you should get the plain tub, not the lemon. If you still see it happen, tell us the product name.

Every correction you make goes into improving this, so they're worth making. And if you'd rather just tell us, email support@cartchkr.com.au with the item name — a human reads all of it.

Why does an out-of-stock item show no price? #

Because a price you can't actually buy at isn't a saving. If the other store is out of that product, CartChkr shows "Out of stock at {store} — not counted toward your saving" and leaves it out of the total rather than quoting you a number that would collapse at checkout.

We also don't pick a substitute for you. What's an acceptable swap is your call, not ours — so tap Wrong match? if you want to choose one.

This matters more than it sounds, because Coles prints a perfectly normal-looking price on products it can't sell you. Reading the page yourself, you'd have no idea.

Fewer items matched than last time — did something break? #

Almost certainly not. We now refuse out-of-stock products at both chains, so some items that used to match and get priced are correctly left alone. Fewer matches, better answers.

You may also see more Check match chips than you used to. Same reason — a size or pack difference that used to pass in silence now says so.

How does Move actually work? #

Tap Move and CartChkr adds the matching product to your cart at the other store, then removes the original. If anything fails partway through it rolls back, so you never end up with an item missing or duplicated.

Move All does the lot at once, and asks first whether you want only the cheaper items — it shows the dollar saving — or the entire cart.

It all happens inside your own signed-in store sessions. There's no separate checkout and no payment to us beyond the one-time unlock. You still check out at the supermarket yourself.

Can I undo a Move? #

If a Move fails, CartChkr undoes it automatically — the item goes back where it was. For a successful Move you've changed your mind about, you'll need to remove it from the new cart and re-add it at the original store. A one-click undo is something we'd like to build, but it isn't in this version.

Where's the microphone button? #

CartChkr uses your keyboard's microphone rather than one of its own. Tap Voice and we open the text box and put the cursor in it — then tap the mic on your own keyboard and talk.

That's a platform limitation, not an oversight: iOS gives apps no way to trigger keyboard dictation, so the honest thing is to hand you to the keyboard that already does it well. On Android, if nothing happens when you tap the keyboard mic, you may need to turn on Voice Typing in your keyboard settings — CartChkr will offer you a shortcut to that screen.

One quirk worth knowing: dictation sometimes drops the commas, so "cheerios, weet-bix, vegemite" can arrive as one long line. CartChkr tries to split those back apart, and you can always edit any line by hand.

Can CartChkr compare ALDI, IGA, or Costco too? #

Not yet — it's Woolworths and Coles, the two chains with comparable online trolleys. ALDI doesn't offer the same online cart, and IGA and Costco vary store by store. New Zealand is next on our list (Pak'nSave and Woolworths NZ), then we'll revisit the Australian options.

Does CartChkr work with Everyday Rewards / Flybuys pricing? #

Yes — and this is one of the better reasons to sign in. When you're signed in at Woolworths with Everyday Rewards and at Coles with Flybuys, CartChkr sees the prices you actually pay. Member specials and reward pricing are baked into the comparison.

It also means two people can legitimately get different answers for the same product on the same day. Yours are the ones that matter.

Pricing & accounts

What it costs, how to manage it

Is CartChkr free? #

Comparing prices is free, forever. That covers a Quick Price Check, building and pricing a CartChkr Cart, receipt scanning, scanning your live store carts, the full item-by-item comparison, and per-unit prices on every item. No payment, no trial, no limit.

A one-time unlock adds the two things that act on the comparison — Move (switching items to the cheaper store) and Price History & Good Buy Analysis. There is no subscription, and nothing recurring:

  • Founding Member — 99¢ one-time, our limited-time early-adopter price
  • Lifetime — $3.99 one-time, full access forever

Either one unlocks both features, on every device signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

How much will I actually save? #

We're not going to quote you an average, because the honest answer is that it depends entirely on what's in your trolley and which specials happen to be running that week. Anyone promising you a fixed figure is guessing.

What we can tell you is why checking is worth it: the published basket comparisons you see in the news track a short list of items in a past month. That tells you about an average store on an average list. It doesn't tell you whether your trolley is cheaper this week — and for most households the cheaper chain genuinely changes week to week. CartChkr answers that specific question in seconds, free, and you can judge the number for yourself.

What is Price History / the "Good Buy" check? #

Open More Price Info on any item to see whether today's price is genuinely good. You get a verdict — Good buy, Usual price, or Seen cheaper — alongside what the item usually costs, the lowest it's been in about the last 60 days, and a small history chart.

It's there to stop two things: "saving" by switching to a price that's quietly high, and paying full attention to a sticker that isn't really a special. We don't tell you what to conclude — we show you the history and let you decide.

It builds up over time, so a product with little history shows "Usual price" until there's enough to say more. When an item is on special we use the store's own declared "was" price, not our own guess. To look up a product's history, the app sends only that product's barcode or store code over a secure connection — never your cart, your account, or anything about you. It's part of the same one-time unlock as Move.

How do I unlock Move and Price History? #

In the app: tap any Move button or open More Price Info. You'll see the two one-time options — Founding or Lifetime — and checkout runs through the App Store or Google Play using the payment method already on your account. Access is immediate.

In the Chrome extension: checkout runs through ExtensionPay, which handles billing via Stripe. Note that the extension and the app are billed separately — they're different stores with different payment systems.

I already paid — how do I restore it on a new phone? #

Open More and tap Restore (it also appears on the unlock screen as "Already paid, or got a code? Restore"). Your unlock lives with your App Store or Google Play account, not with the phone, so it follows you to a new device for free.

If Restore says it can't find a purchase, the usual cause is being signed in to a different App Store or Google Play account from the one that paid — worth checking before anything else, because it's by far the commonest reason. If you're sure it's the right account, email support@cartchkr.com.au with your receipt from Apple or Google and we'll sort it out.

Can I get a refund? #

Yes. Email support@cartchkr.com.au within 14 days and we'll sort you out, no questions asked.

One practical note: purchases made in the app are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds go through them. We'll tell you exactly how to request it — or do it on your behalf where we can. Extension purchases refund through Stripe and usually land within 5-10 business days.

Is there a subscription I need to cancel? #

No. Founding (99¢) and Lifetime ($3.99) are one-time purchases. Nothing recurs, nothing auto-renews, and there's nothing to cancel. You pay once and keep access.

Changed your mind inside 14 days? Email us — see the refund question above.

I paid but Move is still locked #

Tap Restore in More — that re-checks your purchase with Apple or Google directly and is the quickest fix. If that doesn't do it, fully close the app (swipe it away) and reopen it; the entitlement check runs again at launch.

Still locked? Check you're signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account that paid — that's the usual culprit. If it is the right account, email support@cartchkr.com.au with your receipt and we'll activate you manually.

On the Chrome extension: close the side panel completely and reopen it. The licence check runs fresh on every open.

Privacy & data

What we see and don't see

What data does CartChkr collect? #

Your cart stays on your device. What you buy, when you shop, and what's in your trolley is never sent to any server we control. We don't store it, we don't analyse it, and we have nothing to sell. There's no analytics or telemetry either, which is why there's nothing to opt out of.

Two deliberate exceptions, both narrow:

  • Price History. Looking up a product's history sends only that product's barcode or store code over a secure connection to our own price-history service. Not your cart, not your account, not your name or email, not a device identifier. The Privacy Policy names the service and every other company involved.
  • Billing. If you buy the unlock, Apple or Google handles it (ExtensionPay and Stripe on the extension). They see that you paid. They see nothing about your shopping.

Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

Do you see my Woolies or Coles login? #

No. You sign in on each store's own secure page — the real Woolworths and Coles login pages, not a CartChkr imitation of them. Your password never passes through CartChkr, and we never read, store or transmit passwords or auth tokens.

CartChkr uses the session your device already holds with each store, exactly the one you use to shop normally. Both chains also use two-factor codes sent to the account holder, which is a further reason we couldn't use your login even if we wanted to.

How do I delete my data? #

There's very little to delete, because there's very little held. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes your CartChkr Cart, your saved matches and your settings from the device — they were never anywhere else.

If you've bought the unlock and want the billing record removed, email support@cartchkr.com.au and we'll process it within 30 days. There's also a data deletion page if you'd rather use a form.

Is CartChkr affiliated with Woolworths or Coles? #

No. CartChkr is independent, built by an Australian developer, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Woolworths Group Limited or Coles Group Limited. We shop there like everyone else and got sick of comparing prices by hand.

Troubleshooting

When something's not working

I'm overseas or on a VPN and the stores won't load #

Woolworths and Coles restrict their own websites to Australian IP addresses. If you're outside Australia, or connected through a VPN that exits somewhere else, their pages won't load and you'll see an "Access Denied" message — that page is served by the retailer, not by us, and there's nothing CartChkr can do about it.

Turn the VPN off, or set it to an Australian server, and it'll work. If you're genuinely overseas and just want to see how CartChkr works, use Demo Mode in More — it needs no store connection at all.

Coles won't load / I'm being asked to verify I'm human #

You've hit the store's bot detection. Both chains run security checks that occasionally challenge traffic that looks automated, and a scan makes a lot of searches in a short window. It isn't CartChkr being blocked specifically, and it isn't a ban. Fix:

  1. Tap the Coles (or Woolies) tab in CartChkr.
  2. Wait 5-10 seconds for the check to finish, and complete any "verify you're human" step if one appears.
  3. Go back to the CartChkr tab and scan again.

If it happens every shop, sign out and back in at the store — that clears stuck session state. We're still working on making this invisible; for now this nudge is the reliable fix.

Every single item says "no match" #

When it's every item rather than a few, it's almost never matching — it's the other store being unreachable, either bot detection or a maintenance window at the chain's end. Follow the steps above, then scan again.

If you're outside Australia or on a VPN, that's the cause instead.

I keep being asked to sign in again #

Woolworths sessions last around 24 hours, so a sign-in prompt every day or so is normal. Sign in on the Woolies or Coles tab and CartChkr picks the fresh session up by itself.

If you're stuck in a loop where signing in doesn't stick, close the app fully, reopen it, and sign in again from the store tab. And remember a Quick Price Check needs no sign-in at all, if you just want prices now.

Why was the first check after an update so slow? #

Deliberate, and worth the wait. The first time you open CartChkr after an update it re-prices everything from scratch rather than trusting answers worked out by an older version. That's the most expensive thing it ever does, and it's a one-off — subsequent checks are back to normal speed.

Separately, how long a check takes depends on how many items it has to look up — each one is a separate request to the other store. The items we struggle to identify are the slow ones, so improving our matching should speed things up as a side effect.

An item in my CartChkr Cart is still showing an old, wrong match #

Known behaviour, and annoying: a line in your CartChkr Cart keeps the answer it got when you added it, so improvements we ship afterwards don't reach it. If a match looks wrong and the app has updated since you added that item, delete the line and add it again — or tap Clear Cart and rebuild — and it'll be priced fresh.

We're changing this so lines re-price themselves. Until then, re-adding is the fix.

I tapped Move but the item didn't appear in the other cart #

Three things to check, in order:

  1. Are you signed in at the destination store? Moving needs a live session at both ends.
  2. Bot detection. Easily the commonest cause — clear it with the steps above and try again.
  3. Stock. If the product sold out between the scan and the Move, it can't be added. Tap Wrong match? and pick something else.

A failed Move rolls itself back, so nothing is lost. If it fails twice, send us the logs — see below.

Where do my saved and blocked matches live? #

In More, under Saved matches. Everything you've thumbs-upped (saved) or thumbs-downed (blocked) is listed there, and you can undo either. Saved matches persist across every future check, which is why correcting one bad match is worth the ten seconds.

Why is Woolies cheaper one week and Coles the next? #

Because both chains rotate specials constantly — hundreds of items change price every week on supplier deals, promotional calendars and whatever the other one is doing. There's no stable pattern, and for most households the cheaper store genuinely does change week to week.

Which is the whole point. Checking by hand takes twenty minutes you don't have. CartChkr does it in seconds, free, every time.

How do I report a bug or send you logs? #

Open More → Report an issue. That attaches a diagnostic log — recent errors, app version, what the scan did — with no personal or cart detail in it. You don't need to write anything technical; just say what you were trying to do and what happened instead.

In the Chrome extension it's Report Issue in the side panel footer, which offers Gmail, your mail app, or copying the logs to the clipboard.

Either way it reaches support@cartchkr.com.au, you'll get a reference number, and a human replies within 24 hours.
