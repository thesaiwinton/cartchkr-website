Knowledge Base

KB-001 User-side App opens to a blank or stuck screen
▼

The CartChkr tab is empty, a spinner never finishes, or results never appear.

  1. Just updated the app? Give it a minute. The first check after an update re-prices everything from scratch rather than trusting older answers. It's the most expensive thing CartChkr does, and it's a one-off.
  2. Fully close the app — swipe it away in the app switcher — then reopen it.
  3. Still stuck? Tap Clear Cart and start again. That cancels anything still running.
  4. If a blank screen survives a full close and reopen, send us a log: More → Report an issue.
Longer lists have a slower stretch before the rows fill in, because items are priced one at a time. We're making that run several at once.

Chrome extension: close the side panel (toolbar icon) and reopen it; then reload the store tab (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R); then chrome://extensions → CartChkr → refresh.
KB-002 Intermittent Wrong price shown for an item
▼

A price in CartChkr doesn't match what you see on the store's own site or app.

  1. Check which price you're comparing. CartChkr shows the pack price and the per-unit price (e.g. "$1.29 / 100g"). The per-unit figure is for comparing products of different sizes — it isn't what you'll be charged.
  2. Check you're signed in to the same store account in CartChkr and on the website. Member pricing is often below shelf price, so signed-in and signed-out give different answers — both correct.
  3. If the item is in your CartChkr Cart and you've updated the app since adding it, delete that line and add it again. Lines keep the answer they were given when added.
  4. Still wrong? Email us the item name, what CartChkr showed, what the store showed, and which store — plus a log from More → Report an issue.
We rebuilt per-unit pricing in October. It now agrees with Woolworths' own published cup price 99.3% of the time across 5,738 products, up from 96.3%. If you see one out by a factor of ten, we definitely want to hear about it.
KB-003 Known limitation Item shows "no match" but it exists at the other store
▼

An item says "no match" even though you can find it at the other chain yourself.

  1. Tap "Wrong match?" on that item. You'll get the closest alternatives we found — and when there genuinely are none, the button becomes Search {store} store so you can look yourself.
  2. Pick the right product there and we remember it for every future check. One correction, fixed for good.
  3. If you typed the item, add a word or two. "chicken breast fillet 500g" works much better than "chicken breast" — see "A one or two word item picks the wrong thing" in the Knowledge Base for why.
Being straight with you: "no match" currently covers two different things and the wording doesn't tell them apart — either we couldn't find the product, or we found it but couldn't compare the sizes cleanly enough to put a dollar figure on it. We know, and we're fixing the wording.

If every item says no match rather than a few, that's a different problem — see the bot-detection article in the Knowledge Base.
KB-004 Known limitation Wrong product matched
▼

CartChkr matched your item to a different product, brand, flavour or size.

  1. Tap "Wrong match?" and choose the right product. CartChkr saves the pairing and uses it on every future check.
  2. Thumbs-down blocks a bad pairing so it can't come back. Thumbs-up locks a good one in.
  3. Everything you've saved or blocked is listed in More → Saved matches, with an undo on each.
If you asked for something plain and got a flavoured version, that's the one we fixed on 30 October 2026. Ask for plain yoghurt and you should get the plain tub. The weaker spot now is short wording, where a lot of products share the same words (see "A one or two word item picks the wrong thing" in the Knowledge Base). Every correction you make goes into improving this, so they're worth the ten seconds.
KB-005 Intermittent Move didn't work / item not added at the other store
▼

You tapped Move or Move All, it looked like it ran, but the item isn't in the other cart.

  1. Check you're signed in at the destination store. Open the Woolies or Coles tab in CartChkr — moving needs a live session at both ends, not just the one you scanned.
  2. Clear any bot check. Easily the commonest cause — stay on that store tab for 5–10 seconds, complete any "verify you're human" step, then come back and try again.
  3. It may have sold out between the check and the Move. Tap Wrong match? and pick something else.
A failed Move rolls itself back automatically, so nothing is ever lost or duplicated. If it fails twice, send a log (More → Report an issue) with the item name and which direction you were moving.
KB-006 Intermittent A check failed, errored, or returned nothing
▼

A check starts and stops, shows an error, or finishes with no results at all.

  1. Scanning a live store cart? Open the Woolies or Coles tab, check the tile says "Signed in", and make sure there's at least one item in that store's cart.
  2. If everything is unmatched rather than some things, the other store is unreachable, not broken matching — see the bot-detection article in the Knowledge Base, or KB-012 if you're overseas or on a VPN.
  3. Skip sign-in entirely. A Quick Price Check — photo, receipt, typed or dictated list — uses live prices and needs no store session at all.
Occasionally a chain takes its own site down for maintenance. From inside CartChkr that looks identical to "nothing matched", which is on us to make clearer.
KB-007 User-side Where do I start? Can't find a feature
▼

Installed CartChkr but not sure what to do, or can't find something.

  1. CartChkr tab — where everything happens. Quick Price Check at the top (Scan a Photo, Type it or Say it), and below it Check your live Woolies or Coles carts.
  2. Woolies and Coles tabs — where you sign in, and where your real carts live.
  3. More — Saved matches, Restore a purchase, Demo Mode, Report an issue.
Want a look before signing in to anything? More → Demo Mode walks the whole flow on a sample 32-item cart with no login and no store connection.

Chrome extension: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, pin CartChkr, open Woolies or Coles, then click the CartChkr icon — the side panel opens on the right.
KB-008 Working as intended Prices changed after signing in with Everyday Rewards / Flybuys
▼

Prices differ from before you signed in — or from what someone else sees for the same product.

  1. This is correct, and it's the point. CartChkr shows the prices you actually pay. Member pricing is often below shelf price, so signing in changes the numbers.
  2. Stay signed in to Woolworths with Everyday Rewards and Coles with Flybuys, and the comparison reflects real money rather than list prices.
It also means two people can legitimately get different answers for the same product on the same day. Yours are the ones that matter.
KB-009 Known limitation Blocked by store bot detection / "verify you're human"
▼

Items fail to compare, a human-verification screen appears, or a store tab won't load.

  1. Tap the Coles (or Woolies) tab inside CartChkr.
  2. Wait 5–10 seconds for the check to finish, and complete any verification step that appears.
  3. Go back to the CartChkr tab and check again.
  4. Happening every shop? Sign out and back in at the store — that clears stuck session state.
Both chains run security checks that occasionally challenge traffic looking automated, and a check makes a lot of searches in a short window. It isn't a ban, and it isn't CartChkr being singled out. We're still working on making this invisible — we won't pretend it's solved.
KB-010 User-side Paid but Move is still locked / restoring on a new phone
▼

You've paid but Move or Price History still shows the unlock prompt — or you've got a new phone and want your purchase back.

  1. More → Restore. It's also on the unlock screen as "Already paid, or got a code? Restore". This re-checks with Apple or Google directly and is the fastest fix.
  2. If that doesn't do it, fully close the app and reopen — the check runs again at launch.
  3. Check which store account you're signed in to. Being signed in to a different App Store or Google Play account from the one that paid is by far the commonest cause.
  4. Right account and still locked? Email us your Apple or Google receipt and we'll activate you manually.
Your unlock lives with your App Store or Google Play account, not the handset, so it follows you to a new phone for free. Note the app and the Chrome extension are billed separately — different stores, different payment systems — so buying one doesn't unlock the other.

Chrome extension: close the side panel completely and reopen it. The licence check runs on every open.
KB-011 Confirmed bug A one or two word item picks the wrong thing
▼

A short typed or dictated item comes back wrong — or not at all — while adding a word or two to the same item works fine.

  1. Tap "Wrong match?" and pick the right product. The pairing sticks for next time.
  2. More words help a lot right now. Add a brand, a size, or a form: "500g chicken breast fillets" rather than "chicken breast".
We know exactly what causes this and it's our top engineering priority. Our matching was built to compare one full product name against another, and we've been using it unchanged to compare the handful of words you type against a full product name — so short items score far too low and get thrown away. Real example: "chicken breast fillet" matched correctly while plain "chicken breast" was rejected outright. We're not going to promise a date, but this is the thing we're working on.
KB-012 Known limitation Overseas or on a VPN — the stores won't load
▼

"Access Denied", store tabs that won't load, nothing can be checked.

  1. Turn your VPN off, or set it to an Australian server, and try again.
  2. Genuinely overseas? Use More → Demo Mode — it walks the full flow with no store connection at all.
Woolworths and Coles restrict their own websites to Australian IP addresses. That "Access Denied" page is served by the retailer, not by us, and there's nothing CartChkr can do about it. CartChkr is published for Australia only.
KB-013 Confirmed bug An item in my CartChkr Cart keeps an old, wrong answer
▼

An item you added a while back still shows a match or price that looks stale — and deleting it and adding it again gives a better answer.

  1. Delete that line and add the item again. It'll be priced fresh.
  2. If several lines look stale, tap Clear Cart and rebuild.
Known behaviour, and annoying: a line in your CartChkr Cart keeps the answer it got when you added it, so matching improvements we ship afterwards don't reach it. We're changing this so lines re-price themselves. Until then, re-adding is the fix.
KB-014 Working as intended Out-of-stock item shows no price — and fewer items matched
▼

An item reads "Out of stock at {store} — not counted toward your saving", or fewer items matched than on a previous check.

  1. This is deliberate. A price you can't buy at isn't a saving, so we don't quote you a number that would collapse at checkout.
  2. We won't pick a substitute for you — what's an acceptable swap is your call. Tap Wrong match? if you want to choose one.
  3. Fewer matches than before is expected and correct, not a regression. Same if you're seeing more "Check match" chips: a size or pack difference that used to pass in silence now says so.
This matters more than it sounds. Coles prints a perfectly normal-looking price on products it can't actually sell you — reading the website yourself, you'd have no idea.
KB-015 Known limitation Where's the microphone? Voice doesn't do anything
▼

You tapped Voice expecting to start talking, and nothing listened.

  1. Tap Voice, then tap the microphone on your own keyboard and talk. CartChkr opens the text box and puts the cursor in it; your keyboard does the listening.
  2. On Android, if the keyboard mic does nothing, turn on Voice Typing in your keyboard settings. CartChkr offers a shortcut to that screen when it spots the problem.
This is a platform limitation rather than an oversight — iOS gives apps no way to trigger keyboard dictation, so the honest thing is to hand you to the keyboard that already does it well. One quirk: dictation drops commas, so three items can arrive as one line. We try to split them back apart, and every line is editable by hand.
KB-016 Known limitation A check was unusually slow
▼

A check took far longer than usual — particularly the first one after an app update.

  1. The first check after an update is slower by design, and it's a one-off. CartChkr re-prices everything from scratch rather than trusting answers worked out by an older version. The next check is back to normal.
  2. How long it takes depends on how many items there are — each one is its own lookup at the other store. Leave CartChkr open and it'll work through them.
The slow items are the ones we struggle to identify: a wrong guess sends us on an expensive hunt that can't succeed. So the matching work in KB-011 should speed this up as a side effect.
KB-017 User-side Refunds — and there's no subscription to cancel
▼

You want your money back, or you're looking for a subscription to cancel.

  3. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so the refund goes through them — we'll tell you exactly how, or do it for you where we can. Extension purchases refund through Stripe in 5–10 business days.
If something fell short we'd like to know, but it isn't a condition of the refund.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CartChkr free? ▼

Free Comparing prices is free, forever. That covers a Quick Price Check, building and pricing a CartChkr Cart, receipt scanning, scanning your live Woolies or Coles cart, the full item-by-item comparison, and per-unit prices on every item. No payment, no trial, no limit.

Paid A one-time unlock adds the two things that act on the comparison — Move (switching items to the cheaper store) and Price History & Good Buy Analysis. There's no subscription:

  • Founding Member — 99¢ one-time (limited-time early adopter price)
  • Lifetime — $3.99 one-time, full access forever

Either one unlocks both features, on every device signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

What is the CartChkr Cart? ▼

A shopping list that knows what things cost at both stores — so you can compare a shop before you've done it, instead of filling a trolley at one chain and hoping.

Four ways to fill it, and you can mix them freely: scan a receipt and the whole shop comes in at once; photograph a product and we read the label; type a list ("milk 2L, bread, a dozen eggs"); or tap Voice and dictate it.

Every line gets priced at Woolies and at Coles with a running subtotal for each, so you can see which store wins overall. Pick a Preferred Store and Move All loads items into that store's real cart when you're ready — either just the cheaper ones or the lot. Building and pricing it is free; moving items into a store cart is part of the paid unlock.

How does CartChkr compare prices? ▼

Either from a list you give it, or from a cart you've already filled. For a Quick Price Check it takes your items — photo, receipt, typed or dictated — and prices each one at both chains. For a live cart it reads your trolley using your own signed-in session and looks up each item at the other store.

Prices are live, and include any member specials, Everyday Rewards pricing or Flybuys discounts you're entitled to. Your cart stays on your device — CartChkr never sends your cart contents, login details or personal information to any server of ours. There are two narrow exceptions, billing and Price History, both spelled out in the privacy answer below.

What do the grey chips on an item mean? ▼

They're how CartChkr tells you it isn't certain, rather than quietly pretending it is. You'll only ever see one per item — the most important one.

  • Check match — general doubt: we may have the wrong product entirely. It deliberately doesn't claim to know what's wrong, because it doesn't.
  • Check quantity — you asked for several and the match is a multipack. Ask for 6 bananas, match a 5-pack, that's 30 bananas. We say so rather than multiply silently.
  • Size may differ — right product, different amount. If we could normalise the sizes it still counts toward your saving; if we couldn't, it says "not counted" and we leave it out.
  • Different brand — that chain stocks none of your brand at all. A difference in who made it, not necessarily in what you pay.

Tap an item to see the reason spelled out. If a chip is on something we got wrong, tap Wrong match? and pick the right product — we'll remember it.

Does CartChkr work with Everyday Rewards and Flybuys? ▼

Yes, and it's one of the better reasons to sign in. When you're signed in at Woolworths with Everyday Rewards and at Coles with Flybuys, CartChkr sees the prices you actually pay — member specials and reward pricing are baked into the comparison, not the general shelf price.

It also means two people can legitimately get different answers for the same product on the same day. Yours are the ones that matter.

Why does an out-of-stock item show no price? ▼

Because a price you can't actually buy at isn't a saving. If the other store is out of that product, CartChkr says so and leaves it out of your total rather than quoting a number that would collapse at checkout. We also don't pick a substitute for you — what's an acceptable swap is your call, so tap Wrong match? if you want to choose one.

This matters more than it sounds: Coles prints a perfectly normal-looking price on products it can't sell you. Reading the website yourself, you'd have no idea.

Is my data private? Does CartChkr collect anything? ▼

Your cart stays on your device. What you buy, when you shop and what's in your trolley is never sent to any server we control. There's no analytics or telemetry either, which is why there's nothing to opt out of. We never see your Woolies or Coles password — you sign in on each store's own secure page.

Two deliberate exceptions, both narrow. Price History sends only that product's barcode or store code over a secure connection to our own price-history service — not your cart, account, name or device. Billing goes through Apple or Google (ExtensionPay and Stripe on the extension); they see that you paid, and nothing about your shopping. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

Why is Woolies cheaper one week and Coles the next? ▼

Both chains rotate specials constantly — hundreds of items change price every week on supplier deals, promotional calendars, and whatever the other one is doing. There's no stable pattern, and for most households the cheaper store genuinely does change week to week.

Which is the whole point. The published basket comparisons you see in the news track a short list of items in a past month. That doesn't tell you whether your trolley is cheaper this week. Checking that by hand takes twenty minutes; CartChkr does it in seconds, free.

What does CartChkr run on? ▼

Phone — the main way to use it. A free app for iPhone and Android, available in Australia. Everything on this page works there.

Desktop. A Chrome extension that also runs in Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera and Vivaldi. It's a smaller tool: Woolies-to-Coles only, one direction, with no CartChkr Cart and no Price History. It doesn't run on Safari or Firefox, or in any mobile browser — Chrome for iOS and Android don't support extensions at all. Firefox support is on the roadmap.

Both stores restrict their own websites to Australian IP addresses, so you'll need to be in Australia and off a VPN — see the overseas/VPN article in the Knowledge Base.

Can CartChkr compare ALDI, IGA or Costco? ▼

Not yet — it's Woolworths and Coles, the two chains with comparable online trolleys. ALDI doesn't offer the same online cart, and IGA and Costco vary store by store. New Zealand is next on our list (Pak'nSave and Woolworths NZ), then we'll revisit the Australian options.

Is there a subscription I need to cancel? ▼

No. Founding (99¢) and Lifetime ($3.99) are one-time purchases. Nothing recurs, nothing auto-renews, and there's nothing to cancel.

Changed your mind within 14 days? Email support@cartchkr.com.au and we'll refund you, no fuss. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google so the refund goes through them — we'll tell you exactly how.

I paid but Move is still locked — what do I do? ▼

Tap Restore in More — that re-checks your purchase with Apple or Google directly and is the quickest fix. If that doesn't do it, fully close the app and reopen it; the check runs again at launch.

Still locked? Check you're signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account that paid — that's by far the commonest cause. If it is the right account, email support@cartchkr.com.au with your receipt and we'll activate you manually. On the Chrome extension, close the side panel completely and reopen it.

Is CartChkr affiliated with Woolworths or Coles? ▼

No. CartChkr is independent, built by an Australian developer, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Woolworths Group Limited or Coles Group Limited. We shop there like everyone else and got sick of comparing prices by hand.
